## 1.2.0 — 6 October 2026
- FluenTTY now tells you when a new version is out and can install it for you. You'll find the options in Settings › Updates, including a switch to turn update checks off.
- After an update, a short list like this one shows what changed. You can turn it off if you'd rather not see it.
- Your settings, saved sessions and host keys are backed up automatically before every update.

## 1.1.0 — 2 October 2026
- With lots of tabs open, hover over a tab to close it. If the tabs don't all fit, scroll the tab bar with your mouse wheel.
- The title bar buttons now line up neatly.
- The sessions list and the terminal sit on rounded cards, with a little breathing room around the tabs.
- New crab screensaver: little crabs wander across idle terminals, like MobaXterm's penguins. Set it up or turn it off in Settings › Screensaver.

- First release.
